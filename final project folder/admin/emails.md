Dear Sir/Madam,

We are civil-engineering students at IIT Bombay working, under Prof. Basudev Biswal, on flood mapping of the
Mahanadi delta with satellite radar images. For academic use only, we request:

1. Daily discharge and water level at Naraj (and Mundali, if available), June–October 2017–2025;

the resulting maps and the list of embankment reaches that show recurrent flooding behind the bank.
